# Blue House: A service finder Application 
Designed for individuals who lost their job during pandemic as well as to those who are looking for workers to do in-house work that is not in their area of expertise. The application provides an opportunity to workers as they can accept requests from the clients. It also allows them to put their skills or service to use, along with earning income. The application includes rating and comment feature, this is to give suggestions which to pick to the clients and also improvement to the quality of work of the workers.
